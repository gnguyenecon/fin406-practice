How to use this set

Work each problem on paper with your financial calculator, then type your answers and press Check.

New numbers regenerates a problem with different values. Explanation shows the full working for the numbers currently on screen.

The shaded Concept check at the end of a problem is to help improve your conceptual understanding of the problem.

If you spot an error in any of the solutions, please email me (gxn13@psu.edu) as soon as possible and include a screenshot. The numbers change every time a problem is regenerated, so without one I cannot see the version you were looking at.

Before you start

  • Percent boxes take percent: type 7.20, not 0.0720.
  • Prices are in dollars on a $1,000 face value. Type 1054.32, with or without the comma.
  • Duration and modified duration are in years, to 4 decimal places.
  • Negative answers may be typed either way: -14.10 or (14.10).
  • Do not round intermediate calculations. Round each final answer as the question asks.
